# 🧾 **Refund Policy**

## **1. Overview**
We strive to provide a smooth and reliable food delivery experience. If your order is affected by service issues, incorrect items, or quality concerns, you may be eligible for a refund as outlined below.

---

## **2. Eligibility for Refunds**
Refunds may be issued in the following situations:

### **a. Order Not Delivered**
- Delivery partner was unable to reach the customer  
- Order marked as delivered but not received  

### **b. Incorrect or Missing Items**
- Items delivered do not match the order  
- One or more items are missing  

### **c. Quality Issues**
- Food is spoiled, stale, or unsafe to consume  
- Packaging is damaged or leaking  

### **d. Restaurant‑Initiated Cancellation**
- Restaurant unable to prepare the order  
- Item unavailable or out of stock  

---

## **3. Non‑Refundable Situations**
Refunds will *not* be provided for:

- Dissatisfaction with taste when food is prepared correctly  
- Delays caused by traffic, weather, or other external factors  
- Incorrect delivery address or unreachable customer  
- Requests made after the refund window has expired  

---

## **4. Refund Request Window**
Refund requests must be submitted **within 24 hours** of delivery or cancellation.  
Requests made after this period may not be eligible.

---

## **5. Refund Process**
1. Go to the **Help & Support** section in the app.  
2. Select the affected order and choose **Report an Issue**.  
3. Provide details and supporting evidence (photos, description).  
4. Our support team will review the request within **24–48 hours**.  
5. If approved, the refund will be processed as per the method below.

---

## **6. Refund Methods**
Refunds may be issued as:

- **Wallet credit** (fastest)  
- **Original payment method** (UPI, card, net banking)  
- **Promo credit** (in select cases)

Refund timelines depend on your bank or payment provider.

---

## **7. Partial Refunds**
If only part of the order is affected (e.g., one item missing), a **partial refund** may be issued.

---

## **8. Fraud or Abuse**
We reserve the right to deny refunds in cases of repeated misuse, fraudulent claims, or policy violations.

---
