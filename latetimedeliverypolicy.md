

# 🚚 **Late Delivery Policy**

## **1. Overview**
We work closely with our restaurant partners and delivery executives to ensure your order reaches you on time. However, delays may occur due to traffic, weather, high order volume, or operational challenges. This policy explains how late deliveries are handled and when compensation may apply.

---

## **2. What Counts as a Late Delivery**
An order is considered **late** when:

- It is delivered **significantly after** the estimated delivery time shown in the app  
- The delay is caused by operational issues within our control  
- The delivery partner has not moved toward the destination for an extended period  

*Note: External factors such as rain, festivals, traffic restrictions, or natural events may extend delivery timelines.*

---

## **3. Compensation for Late Delivery**
Depending on the situation, you may be eligible for:

- **Delivery fee waiver**  
- **Promo credit or coupon**  
- **Partial refund** (in select cases)  
- **Wallet credit** for future orders  

Compensation is determined after reviewing the order details and delay reason.

---

## **4. Situations Not Eligible for Compensation**
Compensation will not be provided when delays are caused by:

- Severe weather conditions  
- Traffic jams, roadblocks, or public events  
- Incorrect or incomplete delivery address  
- Customer not responding to calls or messages  
- High‑demand peak hours where restaurants take longer to prepare food  

---

## **5. How to Report a Late Delivery**
1. Open the **Help & Support** section in the app  
2. Select the affected order  
3. Choose **Delivery Delay**  
4. Provide any additional details if needed  

Our support team will review your request and respond within **24–48 hours**.

---

## **6. Delivery Partner Tracking**
You can track your delivery partner in real time. If you notice unusual delays or no movement, you may:

- Contact the delivery partner through the app  
- Report the issue via the **Help** section  

---

## **7. Repeated Delays**
If you experience frequent delays in your area, our team will:

- Investigate local operational issues  
- Work with restaurant partners to improve preparation time  
- Adjust delivery partner routing if needed  

---
